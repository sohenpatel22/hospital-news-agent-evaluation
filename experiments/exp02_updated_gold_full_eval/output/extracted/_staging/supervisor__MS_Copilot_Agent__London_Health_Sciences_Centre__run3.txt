London Health Sciences Centre → David Musyj, Supervisor | Hospital governance, operations, management practices, and concerning financial performance identified through a third-party review | On the recommendation of the Minister of Health, Ontario’s Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed David Musyj of LaSalle as Supervisor for London Health Sciences Centre under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The appointment was made because the Lieutenant Governor in Council considered provincial supervision to be in the public interest. Ministry statements reported by CBC specified that Musyj was to work with LHSC leadership and staff on concerns involving hospital governance, operations, management practices, and financial performance identified through a third-party review. LHSC’s full board stepped down as the organization moved under supervision. [ontario.ca], [cbc.ca], [lhsc.on.ca] | September 24, 2024. The appointment was publicly communicated by LHSC and reported in the media on September 25, 2024; the Order in Council was published October 9, 2024.
