I found a relatively concentrated set of documented governance and executive transitions at Arnprior Regional Health (ARH) during the requested period. I treated “leadership” as the CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair, and other senior executive positions where ARH itself documented a substantive appointment/change; I did not count ordinary managerial promotions unless they represented a senior-team transition.
Arnprior Regional Health — leadership, CEO and Board changes
Period: July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026
Additional governance/leadership observations
1. The most consequential executive sequence is 2021–2025. ARH moved from Eric Hanna to Leah Levesque in October 2021, then from Levesque to Jeremy Stevenson in August 2024. (Arnprior Regional Health)
2. Medical leadership also changed twice during the period. Dr. Christine Schriver’s 11-year tenure as Chief of Staff ended with Dr. Florin Padeanu’s appointment effective January 1, 2022; Padeanu subsequently yielded the position to Dr. Kathi Kovacs under the joint ARH/QCH model effective August 1, 2025. (Arnprior Regional Health)
3. Board governance underwent substantial renewal. The June 2022 cycle saw five long-serving directors leave; the 2023 cycle replaced two further directors; and the 2025 cycle saw three more departures. The Board Chair also changed from Cathy Jordan to Oliver Jacob in 2024. (Arnprior Regional Health)
4. ARH’s governance model was itself tightened during the period. In June 2022 the Board proposed reducing the elected Board composition from 12 to 10 directors, and in June 2023 ARH finalized ONCA-compliant by-laws defining a Board structure of 10 elected directors plus ex-officio non-voting CEO, Chief of Staff, President of Medical Staff and CNE positions. This is a governance-structure change rather than an individual leadership change, but it is relevant to performance-management oversight. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Completeness caveat
I would characterize the list above as the exhaustive set of publicly documented substantive leadership/CEO/Board changes I could substantiate from ARH’s website, Board minutes, annual reports and corroborating local/health-sector records for July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026. ARH does not appear to publish a single historical Board-roster archive for every AGM, so some director appointment dates—particularly the three directors now appearing on the post-2025 Board—can be established from before/after rosters but not tied to an exact public appointment date.
Also, ARH’s August 2025 organizational chart shows Oliver Jacob as Board Chair, Jeremy Stevenson as President & CEO, Dr. Kathi Kovacs as Chief of Staff, Raeline McGrath as VP Patient Care/CNE, Jeff Letourneau as CFO, Andrea McClymont as VP HR, and Jennifer Powley as VP LTC & Community Programs, providing a useful post-transition baseline. (Arnprior Regional Health)
For performance-management purposes, the highest-impact transition points are therefore: Oct. 2021 CEO change; Jan. 2022 Chief of Staff change; Jun. 2022 major Board renewal; Jul. 2023 Board renewal; Jul./Aug. 2024 Chair and CEO succession; Aug. 2025 joint Chief of Staff implementation; and 2025 Board renewal.
