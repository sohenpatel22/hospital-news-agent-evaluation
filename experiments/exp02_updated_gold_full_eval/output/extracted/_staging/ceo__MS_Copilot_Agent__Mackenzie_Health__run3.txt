Mackenzie Health leadership and governance changes
Coverage: July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026, newest to oldest
Boundary and completeness notes
The appointment of Fay Lim-Lambie as Board Chair occurred July 14, 2021 and was announced July 15, 2021, immediately before the requested start date of July 16, 2021. I therefore excluded it as an in-period change. A July 27, 2021 Mackenzie Health newsletter referred to the new Chair and Vice-Chair, but did not create a later appointment date. [mackenziehealth.ca], [mackenziehealth.ca]
I found no verified CEO change between July 16, 2021 and March 2025. Altaf Stationwala remained the named President and CEO throughout the 2022 and 2023 governance records. [mackenziehealth.ca], [mackenziehealth.ca]
The 2026 Annual Meeting notice says directors were to be elected on June 18, 2026, but the publicly retrieved notice does not state the election results. The May 28, 2026 agenda records planned board reappointments, not completed changes. I have therefore not inferred any 2026 director additions or departures. [mackenziehealth.ca], [mackenziehealth.ca]
The hospital’s public sources provided substantially stronger evidence than mainstream-media sources for individual board and officer changes. The CEO appointment was independently reported elsewhere, but the official Mackenzie Health announcement contained the most direct and complete record. [canhealth.com], [mackenziehealth.ca]
