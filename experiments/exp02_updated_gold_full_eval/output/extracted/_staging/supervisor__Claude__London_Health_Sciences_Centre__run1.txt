The Public Hospitals Act allows Ontario's health minister to recommend a supervisor when it is in the public interest, typically where there are concerns about administration quality, financial handling, or care quality, and a supervisor takes on all board powers and reports directly to the minister. Musyj was required to provide regular updates to the Ministry of Health and Ontario Health, leading to a final report intended to restore governance structures and stabilize operations. As of mid-2026, Musyj remained in the Supervisor role, with LHSC only then opening applications to recruit a renewed Board of Directors as a step toward ending supervision.
