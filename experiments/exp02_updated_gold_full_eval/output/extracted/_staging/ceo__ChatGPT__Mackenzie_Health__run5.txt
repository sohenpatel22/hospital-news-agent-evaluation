Below is the most comprehensive public-record reconstruction I could substantiate for Mackenzie Health from July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I treated “leadership changes” as material changes involving the President/CEO, Board Chair/officers/directors, Chief of Staff, CNE/clinical executive leadership, and other senior executive roles publicly reported by Mackenzie Health or credible healthcare-transition sources. I excluded routine annual re-elections where there was no change in board membership or leadership role, and I did not include Mackenzie Health Foundation-only changes unless they also affected the hospital board.
Mackenzie Health comprises Mackenzie Richmond Hill Hospital and Cortellucci Vaughan Hospital. (Mackenzie Health)
Executive-transition timeline
For performance-management purposes, the major succession sequence is:
Altaf Stationwala — President & CEO through June 27, 2025 → Mary-Agnes Wilson — Interim President & CEO beginning July 2025 → Carmine Stumpo — President & CEO beginning April 13, 2026. (Mackenzie Health)
The medical-leadership sequence is similarly material: Dr. Steven Jackson → Dr. David Rauchwerger as Chief of Staff, effective December 15, 2025. (Mackenzie Health)
The governance succession is: Mark Falbo → Fay Lim-Lambie → Stephanie Zee → John Fursey as Board Chair between July 2021 and June 2025. (Mackenzie Health)
Important completeness note
I would characterize the table as exhaustive of material, publicly documented leadership/governance changes I could substantiate, rather than asserting that it captures every internal personnel movement. Mackenzie’s public archive does not appear to publish a single historical executive-roster change log, and some appointments—particularly mid-level executive, medical-department and professional-staff appointments—are not consistently announced in news releases.
I also did not count routine board re-elections as “changes” unless they altered the composition or governance leadership. For example, the 2022 and 2024 minutes contain numerous director reappointments, but those do not represent turnover. (Mackenzie Health)
From an Ontario Health performance-management perspective, the most consequential governance events in the period are therefore the 2021 board reset, 2023 director resignation/new director cycle, 2024 Chair transition, 2025 CEO succession and Board reset, 2025 Chief of Staff succession, CHRO transition, and April 2026 permanent CEO transition.
