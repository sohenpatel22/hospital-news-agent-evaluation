Hospital: Renfrew Victoria Hospital (RVH), Renfrew, Ontario
Issue: Financial irregularities and governance failures involving RVH's relationship with a related entity, Renfrew Health (RH), used to channel undisclosed executive compensation.
Paragraph blurb: In June 2024, the Ontario Ministry of Health appointed Altaf Stationwala, then President and CEO of Mackenzie Health, as supervisor of Renfrew Victoria Hospital under the Public Hospitals Act. The appointment followed financial issues identified during a review by Ontario Health and an independent third party, and Stationwala was tasked with strengthening the hospital's governance, accountability, and ability to deliver care. Unlike most previous supervisor appointments, this one was not preceded by a formal Investigator Report; instead, a confidential KPMG review commissioned by Ontario Health served that function, with the supervisor's early work incorporating investigator-style fact-finding. The appointment was announced on June 27, 2024, and the supervisor's mandate commenced July 8, 2024. His final report, released in June 2025, documented a disturbing misallocation of millions of healthcare dollars — mostly through double compensation, a $1 million interest-free loan, a $1.3 million duplicate pension arrangement, and other perks paid to a former CEO, totaling over $1.6 million on top of RVH salary, plus nearly $3 million in executive compensation funneled through Renfrew Health over 11 years with no demonstrated RH roles or accountabilities. Stationwala remained at RVH working alongside incoming president and CEO Suzanne Madore until June 20, 2025, marking the end of the supervisory mandate.
