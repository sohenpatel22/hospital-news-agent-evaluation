Renfrew Victoria Hospital | Altaf Stationwala appointed as hospital supervisor following concerns about governance, management and financial practices | On the recommendation of the Minister of Health, the Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed Altaf Stationwala as supervisor of Renfrew Victoria Hospital under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The Order in Council states that the appointment was considered to be in the public interest. Contemporary Ministry information reported by CBC said Stationwala was to work with the hospital’s CEO, board and staff to address governance and management concerns, including financial issues identified through reviews by Ontario Health and an independent third party. His final report later described the initial concern as the relationship between the hospital and the related entity Renfrew Health.
