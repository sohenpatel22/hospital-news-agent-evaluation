Central Region
The internal regional reference classifies Mackenzie Health in Ontario Health’s Central Region. Mackenzie Health comprises Mackenzie Richmond Hill Hospital and Cortellucci Vaughan Hospital, so the governance changes below apply to the hospital corporation rather than to only one campus. Completeness note
This is an exhaustive list of changes I could independently verify from publicly accessible, date-specific sources within July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I reviewed official news releases, annual/post-AGM minutes, board rosters, leadership pages and supplementary health-sector coverage. The public minutes sometimes state that directors or committee appointments were approved “as presented” without reproducing the underlying slate. For example, the June 20, 2022 minutes congratulate newly appointed directors but the retrieved text does not explicitly identify which attendees were newly elected. I therefore did not infer additions or departures merely by comparing attendance lists, since those lists can also vary because of regrets, ex-officio status or absences.
