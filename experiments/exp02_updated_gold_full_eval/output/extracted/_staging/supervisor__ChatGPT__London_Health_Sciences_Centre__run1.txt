London Health Sciences Centre → David Musyj
Hospital: London Health Sciences Centre
Issue: Governance, operational and management concerns, particularly financial performance, following a third-party review; subsequent provincial intervention was also associated with leadership instability and significant financial/governance failures.
Paragraph blurb: On September 24, 2024, Ontario’s Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed David Musyj as Supervisor of London Health Sciences Centre under s. 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health. The Order in Council states that the government considered a supervisor appointment to be in the public interest. The Ministry/LHSC rationale was to address concerns identified through a third-party review involving hospital governance and operations and management practices, including financial performance, while restoring effective governance, improving hospital finances and stabilizing operations. Musyj had already been serving as LHSC’s Acting/Interim President and CEO. The later 2026 Supervisor’s Interim Report characterized the underlying situation more broadly as governance, financial and operational failures, including persistent leadership churn, weak oversight and financial-control problems, while emphasizing that strong clinical performance and patient care had continued. (Ontario)

Continuation through July 16, 2026
There was not a second supervisor appointment during the remainder of the requested period. Instead, Ontario amended the supervisor’s remuneration order on January 29, 2026 to permit remuneration for up to 875 days, explicitly stating that this was to enable the existing Supervisor to complete his mandate. The amendment refers back to Order in Council 1219/2024 and therefore confirms continuity of David Musyj’s appointment rather than a replacement. (Ontario)
The LHSC Supervisor was still in office as of July 16, 2026; LHSC’s July 15, 2026 announcement identifies David Musyj as the continuing provincial Supervisor while a renewed Board was being established. (LHSC)
Exhaustiveness note
For the specified July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026 window, my search of Ontario’s published Orders in Council and Ministry of Health material found one unique government-appointed statutory supervisor: David Musyj. The Ontario Ministry of Health specifically lists the LHSC supervisor appointment as occurring in September 2024, while the 2026 amendment confirms the same appointment continued. (Ontario)
I have not counted ordinary Acting/Interim President & CEO appointments as “government-appointed supervisors,” because they are different roles and do not constitute the statutory supervisory intervention under s. 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. Musyj’s earlier Acting/Interim CEO role therefore does not create an additional supervisor entry. (Ontario)
