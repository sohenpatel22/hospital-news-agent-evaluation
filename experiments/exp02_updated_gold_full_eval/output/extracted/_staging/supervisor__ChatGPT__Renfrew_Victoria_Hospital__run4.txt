Renfrew Victoria Hospital | Governance, accountability and financial-management concerns involving Renfrew Health and former executive practices | Altaf Stationwala was appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council as supervisor of Renfrew Victoria Hospital after the Ministry of Health determined that supervision was in the public interest. The supervisor’s final report says the intervention arose from concerns about the relationship between RVH and related entity Renfrew Health, including the appropriateness of hospital funds transferred to that entity. Stationwala’s review expanded to financial, operational, leadership and governance issues and identified irregular transactions and practices involving certain former executives, while noting that current active executive-team members and the current board were not involved in initiating those irregularities. The appointment was announced June 27, 2024 and commenced July 8, 2024.
