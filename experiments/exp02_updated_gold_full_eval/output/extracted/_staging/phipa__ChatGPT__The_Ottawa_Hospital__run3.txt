The Ottawa Hospital | PHIPA access/correction rights — after-visit summary and statement of disagreement | A complainant asked The Ottawa Hospital to correct portions of an After Visit Summary under PHIPA and later requested that a statement of disagreement be attached to the record under s.55(13). The hospital declined to make the requested corrections because it had no duty to correct the information under s.55(8), but added the statement of disagreement to the patient’s electronic health record. The IPC upheld the hospital’s position, finding that the hospital had met its s.55(13) obligation and was not required to make the statement available through the patient portal. The complaint was dismissed. (IPC Ontario) | January 26, 2026 | Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario — PHIPA Decision 326, Complaint HA23-00094, Jennifer Olijnyk | IPC / PHIPA Decision 326
The Ottawa Hospital | PHIPA access rights — requested EMR screenshots / “samples” | A complainant sought screenshots or “samples” of 25 programmed displays in her electronic medical record, including items described as “Storyboard” and “Snapshot.” The Ottawa Hospital explained that these were programmed EMR displays and offered printed copies of the underlying personal health information, but declined to create screenshots. The IPC held that the requested screenshots could potentially constitute “records” under PHIPA, but the hospital did not create or maintain those screenshots and therefore was not required to create them. The adjudicator found that the hospital had proposed reasonable alternatives for accessing information that did not exist in the requested form. Additional allegations concerning an earlier access request, alleged PHIPA offences, and procedural unfairness were also dismissed. (IPC Ontario) | June 25, 2026 | Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario — PHIPA Decision 340, Jenny Ryu | IPC Latest Decisions — PHIPA Decision 340 listing
