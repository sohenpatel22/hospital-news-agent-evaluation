Assuming you are referring to the distinct institutions Stevenson Memorial Hospital (Alliston) and Renfrew Victoria Hospital (Renfrew), which both underwent provincial interventions during this window, here is the exhaustive list of interim supervisors appointed by the Government of Ontario between July 16, 2021, and July 16, 2026. 
1. Renfrew Victoria Hospital
Hospital: Renfrew Victoria Hospital
Issue: Governance, accountability, and concerning financial practices (specifically regarding business arrangements with a non-profit organization named Renfrew Health). 
Paragraph blurb: Following a review conducted by Ontario Health and an independent third party, the provincial government appointed Altaf Stationwala (President and CEO of Mackenzie Health) as hospital supervisor. He was tasked with working closely with the hospital's CEO, board of directors, and staff to address governance and management gaps while ensuring that clinical services and patient care experienced zero disruptions. 
Date: June 27, 2024 (Effective July 8, 2024)

2. Stevenson Memorial Hospital (First Appointee) 
Hospital: Stevenson Memorial Hospital
Issue: Deficiencies in governance, management, leadership culture, and patient care highlighted in a 77-page investigative report by investigator Janice Skot. 
Paragraph blurb: The Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed Eric Hanna (former President and CEO of Arnprior Regional Health) as supervisor to oversee hospital operations and execute required governance and accountability reforms. Hanna collaborated with local interim leadership to implement structural changes while keeping the hospital's major redevelopment project on track. 
Date: September 26, 2024 
Source: Government of Ontario (Order in Council 1271/2024) / Milton Now

Hospital: Stevenson Memorial Hospital
Issue: Continuation of leadership oversight, financial stabilization, and governance reform after the initial supervisor stepped down.

Stumpo assumed operational control to continue guiding the hospital's accountability metrics and driving its ongoing infrastructure redevelopment initiative.
