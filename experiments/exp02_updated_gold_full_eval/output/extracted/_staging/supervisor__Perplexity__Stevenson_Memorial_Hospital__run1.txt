## Scope and qualification
- Ontario’s **Public Hospitals Act** authorizes the Lieutenant Governor in Council, on the Minister of Health’s recommendation, to appoint a hospital supervisor where it is considered in the public interest. The supervisor can exercise the powers of the hospital board unless the appointment provides otherwise. [ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p40/v26)
- The search identified **Eric Hanna** and **Carmine Stumpo** as the people appointed to the supervisor role within the requested period.
- The sources reviewed do not establish an exact Order in Council number or exact effective date for either appointment. Accordingly, the dates above distinguish between the reported appointment month and the precise publication/reporting dates.
- The term “interim supervisor” appears to describe the temporary nature of the provincial intervention; the Ontario releases themselves generally use **“Supervisor, Stevenson Memorial Hospital”**, rather than “interim supervisor.” [news.ontario](https://news.ontario.ca/en/release/1007130/ontario_breaks_ground_on_stevenson_memorial_hospital_expansion)
