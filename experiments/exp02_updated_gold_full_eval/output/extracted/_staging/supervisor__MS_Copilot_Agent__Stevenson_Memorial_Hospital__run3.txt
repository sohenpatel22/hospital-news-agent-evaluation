Stevenson Memorial Hospital → Governance, management, quality-of-care and financial-performance concerns → Eric Hanna was appointed Supervisor after a provincially ordered investigation identified concerns encompassing governance, leadership, quality and safety of care, clinical services, operations, financial performance, staffing, and organizational stability. The investigator recommended appointment of a supervisor as part of a broader package of 16 recommendations. As Supervisor, Eric Hanna was expected to implement changes responding to those concerns and give progress updates to the Ministry of Health and Ontario Health. His appointment was subsequently revoked when he stepped down and a successor was appointed. Stevenson Memorial Hospital → Continued need for governance, accountability, operational, financial and patient-care oversight → Carmine Stumpo was appointed to succeed Eric Hanna and continue carrying out the Supervisor’s duties. The governing Order in Council revoked Hanna’s appointment at the end of June 8, 2025 and made Stumpo’s appointment effective at the beginning of June 9. Stumpo’s January 2026 interim report states that his work focused on stabilizing leadership and governance, rebuilding operational capacity, addressing quality and access concerns, and developing a regional approach to care. It also confirms the succession history from Hanna to Stumpo. Completeness and date notes
The Government of Ontario’s January 2026 Interim Report Supervisor Stevenson Memorial Hospital gives the relevant appointment chronology: the investigator was appointed in February 2024, Eric Hanna was appointed Supervisor on September 26, 2024, and Carmine Stumpo was appointed by Order in Council on June 5, 2025 to succeed him, with his work beginning June 9. It identifies no other Supervisor in the requested period. [ontario.ca]
The two individual Orders in Council independently confirm those appointments and the succession between them. Order 767/2025 expressly revokes Order 1271/2024, ending Hanna’s appointment on June 8, 2025 and commencing Stumpo’s on June 9, 2025. [ontario.ca], [ontario.ca]
