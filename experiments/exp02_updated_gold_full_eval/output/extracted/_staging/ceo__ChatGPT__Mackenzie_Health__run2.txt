Below is a consolidated, date-bounded review of publicly documented Mackenzie Health leadership, CEO, and Board of Directors changes from July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I treated “leadership changes” as changes involving the President/CEO, Chief of Staff, executive leadership team, Board Chair/vice-chair roles, or additions/removals from the hospital Board. Routine director re-elections with no change in status are not presented as separate “changes.”
Important boundary findings
July 16, 2021 matters. Mackenzie Health announced Fay Lim-Lambie’s appointment as Board Chair on July 15, 2021, one day before the requested start date. Because your requested window is July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026, I have excluded that July 15 event from the main change list rather than treating it as in-range. (Mackenzie Health)
For 2022, Mackenzie Health’s Board minutes document multiple re-elections — Azi Boloorchi, Peter Hargitai, Joby McKenzie, Fay Lim-Lambie and Stephanie Zee — but these were continuations of existing Board service rather than new leadership changes, so I did not inflate the list with routine reappointments. (Mackenzie Health)
Governance picture at the end of the requested period
By the end of the window, Mackenzie Health had moved through a substantial governance and executive succession cycle: Altaf Stationwala → Mary-Agnes Wilson (interim) → Carmine Stumpo at the CEO level; Stephanie Zee → John Fursey at the Board Chair level; and Steven Jackson → David Rauchwerger as Chief of Staff. The 2025 Board refresh also added Heidi Francis, Dr. Kimia Honarmand, Viki Lazaris and Dr. Jun Yuan, while the 2025 transition saw Fay Lim-Lambie and Ruby Philip-Katyal leave the Board. (LinkedIn)
One caveat on “exhaustive”: Mackenzie Health publishes Board agendas/minutes and organizational structures, but not every internal executive appointment or departure is accompanied by a public news release. The list above therefore represents the exhaustive set of publicly verifiable leadership/CEO/Board changes I could identify within the specified date window, rather than a claim that no unannounced internal management changes occurred.
