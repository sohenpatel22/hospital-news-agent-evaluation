Stevenson Memorial Hospital → | Governance, management, patient-care, quality, operational and financial-performance concerns identified by the provincial investigation → | Eric Hanna was appointed Supervisor after investigator Janice Skot’s June 28, 2024 report made 16 recommendations, including appointing a supervisor. The Order in Council states that the Minister of Health recommended the appointment, that the hospital board received the required notice, and that the Lieutenant Governor in Council considered the appointment to be in the public interest. The Ministry said the supervisor would oversee operations, implement changes addressing the investigation’s concerns, and provide updates to the Ministry of Health and Ontario Health. Hanna’s appointment was subsequently revoked when he stepped down and a successor was appointed. Stevenson Memorial Hospital → | Continuation of the hospital’s governance and accountability intervention following Eric Hanna’s decision to step down → | Carmine Stumpo was appointed to succeed Hanna and continue the supervisor’s duties. The Order in Council explicitly says Hanna wished to step down, revokes Hanna’s appointment at the end of June 8, 2025, and appoints Stumpo effective at the beginning of June 9. Subsequent reporting described Stumpo’s mandate as overseeing operations and strengthening governance, accountability, finances and the hospital’s ability to deliver care. Stumpo’s January 2026 interim report states that his work built on earlier efforts and focused on establishing a path forward, defining a vision for the hospital and reporting progress on enabling actions.
