Stevenson Memorial Hospital → Eric Hanna | Governance, management, patient-care quality, accountability and financial concerns identified through a Ministry-appointed investigation | Following a review of concerns about Stevenson Memorial Hospital’s governance, management and quality of care, investigator Janice Skot submitted a final report on June 28, 2024 containing 16 recommendations, including appointment of a hospital supervisor. The Lieutenant Governor in Council subsequently appointed Eric Hanna under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The appointment was intended to implement changes and solutions addressing the identified concerns, with progress updates to the Ministry of Health and Ontario Health. Hanna’s appointment remained in effect until it was revoked at the end of June 8, 2025, when he stepped down. Stevenson Memorial Hospital → Carmine Stumpo | Continuation of supervision to strengthen governance, leadership, accountability, operations, finances and quality of care | After Eric Hanna indicated that he wished to step down, the Minister of Health recommended Carmine Stumpo as his successor. Order in Council 767/2025 revoked Hanna’s appointment effective at the end of June 8, 2025 and appointed Stumpo effective at the beginning of June 9, 2025, under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. Stumpo’s January 2026 interim report says his work built upon the preceding investigation and supervision, while addressing leadership instability, operational capacity, governance, service access and quality-of-care concerns.
