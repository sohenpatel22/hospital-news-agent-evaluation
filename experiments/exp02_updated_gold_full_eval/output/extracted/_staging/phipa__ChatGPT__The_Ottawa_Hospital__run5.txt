The Ottawa Hospital | PHIPA Decision 326 — Correction request and statement of disagreement in an electronic health record | A patient asked The Ottawa Hospital to correct portions of an “After Visit Summary” and, after the hospital declined the requested corrections, asked that a statement of disagreement be attached to the record under PHIPA s.55(13). The IPC found that the hospital did not have a duty under s.55(8) to make the requested corrections and had properly attached the patient’s statement of disagreement to the electronic health record. The adjudicator also found that the hospital was not required to make that statement available through the patient portal, and dismissed the complaint. From a performance-management perspective, the decision is relevant to governance of record-integrity requests, documentation controls, and how patient correction/disagreement workflows are implemented in an EHR environment. The Ottawa Hospital | PHIPA Decision 340 — Access to screenshots of electronic medical-record displays | A patient requested “samples” or screenshots of 25 electronic medical-record displays, including items such as “Storyboard” and “Snapshot.” The Ottawa Hospital offered printed copies of the personal health information that would appear in those displays but declined to provide screenshots. The IPC concluded that screenshots could potentially constitute “records” under PHIPA, but the hospital did not create or maintain the requested screenshots, and therefore was not required to create them for the requester. The adjudicator found that the hospital had proposed reasonable alternatives for providing access to the underlying information and dismissed the complaint, along with additional allegations concerning an earlier access request, alleged PHIPA offences, and procedural unfairness. For hospital-performance oversight, this decision is particularly relevant to EHR transparency, access-request operating procedures, record-production capabilities, and expectations around reproducing information from Epic/other EMR interfaces.
