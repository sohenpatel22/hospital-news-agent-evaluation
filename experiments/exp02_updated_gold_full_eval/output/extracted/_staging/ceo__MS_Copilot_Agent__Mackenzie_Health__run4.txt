I treated “leadership” as changes to the President and CEO, interim CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair and officers, and elected or departing directors. I excluded routine committee-member assignments, unchanged annual reappointments, Foundation-only leadership, and the July 14, 2021 board changes because the underlying appointments occurred two days before your requested start date, even though they were reported later.
Scope and methodology
Ontario Hospitals by Region 1.docx identifies Mackenzie Health as a Central Region hospital organization. [Ontario Ho...ion 1.docx | Word]
I searched official hospital news, open board minutes and agendas, current leadership pages, mainstream/local media, and supplementary health-sector coverage for July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026, inclusive. The entries below are ordered from most recent to oldest and use the requested format.

Completeness note
I found no separately substantiated CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair, officer or elected-director change in the public sources for July 16, 2021 through the end of 2022. The available 2022 minutes and agendas show governance activity and annual officer appointments, but the retrieved material did not explicitly document a qualifying change with enough detail to add a defensible row. Likewise, I did not count temporary role coverage visible only on a current organization chart, because the retrieved source did not specify a public appointment date within the requested period.
