Below is a consolidated, source-checked chronology of Mackenzie Health hospital leadership, CEO, Chief of Staff and Board of Directors changes falling within July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I prioritized Mackenzie Health’s own announcements, board minutes and organizational charts, and used external sources only to corroborate where useful.
Scope note: I treated a “change” as an appointment, departure/resignation, succession to a governance office, or material change in a senior hospital leadership role. Routine annual re-elections/reappointments with no change in office are not listed as separate events. The July 2021 Fay Lim-Lambie appointment is not included as an in-period event because her appointment occurred at the July 14, 2021 AGM and Mackenzie Health announced it July 15, both before the requested July 16 start date. (Mackenzie Health)

Overall governance timeline
The principal governance succession sequence is Fay Lim-Lambie → Stephanie Zee → John Fursey. Because Lim-Lambie’s appointment occurred on July 14, 2021, it falls just outside your requested July 16 start date, while Zee assumed the Chair in June 2024 and Fursey in June 2025. (Mackenzie Health)
The principal CEO sequence inside the period is Altaf Stationwala → Mary-Agnes Wilson (interim) → Carmine Stumpo (permanent), with the transition occurring in mid-2025 and completing in April 2026. (Mackenzie Health)
The principal medical leadership succession is Dr. Steven Jackson → Dr. David Rauchwerger as Chief of Staff, effective December 15, 2025. (Mackenzie Health)
Exclusions: I did not count ordinary three-year Board re-elections/reappointments in 2022–2025 as separate “changes” where the individual simply continued in the same governance role; the Board minutes show such reappointments for members including Azi Boloorchi, Peter Hargitai, Joby McKenzie, Fay Lim-Lambie, Stephanie Zee, Atul Mehta, Deryn Rizzi, David Steinberg, Dev Chopra and Ruby Philip-Katyal. (Mackenzie Health)
For a performance-management perspective, the highest-significance events are the 2025 CEO succession, 2025 Board Chair succession, 2025 Chief of Staff succession, 2025–26 senior-executive restructuring, and 2026 permanent CEO appointment, because those changes affect strategic continuity, governance oversight, clinical leadership and organizational accountability.
