Coverage window: July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026, newest to oldest
Scope and completeness notes
No qualifying July 16 to December 31, 2021 change was located. Bert Clark’s appointment as Board Chair occurred on June 14, 2021, before the requested start date, so it is intentionally excluded from the table. [nygh.on.ca]
The annual reviews provide useful year-end control points: the 2021-2022 report includes the Board and Senior Leadership Team; the 2022-2023 report covers the following year; the 2023-2024 report identifies Bert Clark and Everton Gooden; and the 2024-2025 report identifies Mitch Frazer and Everton Gooden. [nygh.on.ca], [nygh.on.ca], [nygh.on.ca], [nygh.on.ca]
I found no reliable public announcement of a 2023 hospital Board of Governors intake. That does not prove no re-elections or routine term renewals occurred; it means no independently dated, publicly discoverable change announcement was located.
I did not count recruitment postings as completed changes, except the February 2026 posting is retained as context because it explicitly forecast two vacancies that were subsequently filled.
I excluded the five North York General Foundation board appointments announced in June 2025 from the hospital-governance count. They were Sean Cohan, Christine Cruz-Clarke, Ted Garrard, Vince Imerti and Richard So. The Foundation board is separate from NYG’s hospital Board of Governors. [nyghfoundation.ca], [newswire.ca]
