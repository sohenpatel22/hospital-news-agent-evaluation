1
The Ottawa Hospital → Access/correction of an after-visit summary and statement of disagreement → A patient asked The Ottawa Hospital to correct portions of an after-visit summary and subsequently requested that a statement of disagreement be attached to the hospital’s records under PHIPA s.55(13). The hospital declined to make the requested substantive corrections because it found that the statutory duty to correct did not apply, but it did attach the statement of disagreement to the patient’s electronic health record. The IPC upheld the hospital’s position, finding that the hospital had satisfied its obligation to attach the statement and was not required to make the statement accessible through the patient portal. For performance-management purposes, the decision is relevant to TOH’s handling of correction requests, EHR governance, documentation of patient disagreement, and alignment between the underlying record and patient-facing portal functionality. (IPC Ontario)

2
The Ottawa Hospital → Patient request for screenshots of EHR displays under PHIPA → A patient requested “samples” or screenshots of 25 programmed displays in The Ottawa Hospital’s electronic medical record system, including items described as “Storyboard” and “Snapshot.” TOH offered printed copies of the personal health information represented by the displays but declined to produce screenshots. The IPC found that screenshots could potentially constitute “records” under PHIPA, rejecting the hospital’s broader argument on that point, but concluded that TOH did not create or maintain the requested screenshots and therefore had no obligation to create them in the circumstances. The adjudicator found that TOH’s proposed alternatives were reasonable and dismissed the complaint, including additional allegations concerning the hospital’s response to a prior access request and alleged PHIPA offences. The decision is particularly relevant to EHR information architecture, records-access policy, patient portal expectations, and operational definitions of what constitutes a retrievable record. (IPC Ontario)
Date → June 25, 2026
