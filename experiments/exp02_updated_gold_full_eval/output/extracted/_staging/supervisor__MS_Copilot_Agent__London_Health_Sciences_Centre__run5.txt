London Health Sciences Centre → David Musyj appointed Supervisor | Hospital governance, operations, management practices and financial performance | The Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed David Musyj of LaSalle as Supervisor for London Health Sciences Centre under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act, concluding that an appointment was in the public interest. The order took effect when it was made. The Ministry of Health subsequently said Musyj would work with LHSC leadership and staff to address concerns identified through a third-party review regarding hospital governance and operations, management practices and concerning financial performance. At the time, LHSC’s entire board resigned, and CBC reported that the hospital was projecting a $150-million deficit. LHSC’s own announcement described the mandate as strengthening governance, accountability and the organization’s ability to deliver care, while continuing regular hospital operations.
