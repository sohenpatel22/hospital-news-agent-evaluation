Below is a consolidated chronology of publicly documented leadership, CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair, and elected/appointed Board-member changes at Arnprior Regional Health (ARH) from July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I have treated a “change” as an appointment, election/re-election, retirement, resignation/end of term, or material succession change—not merely a person continuing in office.
I found 8 material change events that can be substantiated from ARH records and credible secondary sources in the period. One 2026 CEO transition is particularly recent and is supported by Longwoods’ transition record; ARH’s public website still appears to contain legacy leadership information, so I flag that distinction explicitly.
Important July 2026 development
There is one additional CEO transition that falls exactly within your requested window and should not be missed:
Arnprior Regional Health → Jeremy Stevenson → Pierre Noel, Interim President & CEO — July 14, 2026.
Longwoods’ healthcare-transition database records Pierre Noel as Interim President and CEO of Arnprior Regional Health on July 14, 2026. (Longwoods) Noel was previously President and CEO of Pembroke Regional Hospital, so this represents a major regional leadership transition. Subsequent August 2026 reporting independently identifies Noel as Interim President & CEO of Arnprior Regional Health, corroborating that the transition occurred, although that reporting is outside your July 16 cutoff. (Billy Denault)
For your July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026 window, I would therefore treat July 14, 2026 as an included CEO-change event, even though the strongest public confirmation currently available is the Longwoods transition record rather than a contemporaneous ARH press release. ARH’s indexed “Our Board” page still lists Jeremy Stevenson as President & CEO, indicating that ARH’s public web pages may lag the actual transition. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Overall leadership trajectory
The governance picture over the five-year period is fairly clear: Eric Hanna retired after 13 years and was replaced by Leah Levesque in October 2021; Levesque was replaced by Jeremy Stevenson in August 2024; and Pierre Noel was recorded as Interim President & CEO on July 14, 2026. Medical leadership changed from Dr. Christine Schriver to Dr. Florin Padeanu in January 2022, followed by a joint ARH–QCH Chief of Staff model under Dr. Kathi Kovacs from August 2025. Board governance saw a significant refresh in 2022 and 2023, Cathy Jordan handed the Chair to Oliver Jacob in 2024, and Jordan, Curtis McGrath and Colleen MacPhee subsequently completed their Board service in 2025. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Scope note: I have focused this chronology on hospital/corporate leadership, CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair and elected/appointed Board directors. I have not counted ordinary manager-level hiring, committee-member appointments, or temporary project leadership unless they represented a substantive hospital leadership succession. The ARH public-reporting page confirms that annual reports and audited statements are available for each of the relevant fiscal years, providing an additional governance trail. (Arnprior Regional Health)
