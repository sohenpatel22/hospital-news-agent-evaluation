I found one Government of Ontario hospital-supervisor appointment for Renfrew Victoria Hospital within the requested period, July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I found no earlier or subsequent provincial supervisor appointment for RVH in that window in the Ontario Orders in Council record; the June 2025 order terminates the 2024 appointment. (Ontario)
Appointment and end of mandate
Altaf Stationwala was appointed on June 26, 2024, under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The OIC expressly says the appointment was made because the Lieutenant Governor in Council considered a supervisor appointment to be in the public interest. (Ontario)
The Ministry's final report states that the appointment was announced June 27, 2024 and commenced July 8, 2024. It describes the supervisory mandate as covering four principal dimensions: financial, operations, leadership and governance. (Ontario)
The appointment was subsequently terminated effective 11:59:59 p.m. on June 20, 2025 by Order in Council 766/2025. Thus, Stationwala is the only provincial hospital supervisor appointment I identified for RVH during the requested five-year period. (Ontario)
The Ministry's published health page independently confirms that “In June 2024, Altaf Stationwala was appointed supervisor for Renfrew Victoria Hospital.” (Ontario)
Performance-management significance
The supervisor intervention appears to have been driven principally by financial and governance concerns rather than a general clinical-quality failure. The final report identifies financial irregularities involving the use of hospital funds for the benefit of former executives, alongside concerns requiring remediation in governance, leadership, operating stability and administrative systems. (Ontario)
The report also records concrete improvement work during the supervisory period, including governance-policy updates, investments in financial/HR/scheduling systems, and improvements in diagnostic-imaging access. (Ontario)
Bottom line: 1 supervisor appointment, 1 individual — Altaf Stationwala. No other Ontario government hospital-supervisor appointment for Renfrew Victoria Hospital surfaced for July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026 in the provincial sources reviewed. (Ontario)

Renfrew Victoria Hospital (RVH) | Governance, management and financial-practice concerns; provincial supervisory intervention | The Ontario government appointed Altaf Stationwala, then President and CEO of Mackenzie Health, as supervisor of Renfrew Victoria Hospital under s. 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The subsequent Ministry final supervisor report says the appointment followed ongoing discussions involving RVH, Renfrew Health, Ontario Health East and the Ministry, together with a confidential KPMG independent review.
