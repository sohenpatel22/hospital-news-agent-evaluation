Executive finding
I found one individual appointed by the Government of Ontario as Supervisor of London Health Sciences Centre (LHSC) between July 16, 2021 and July 16, 2026. The formal legal title was**“Supervisor,” not “Interim Supervisor.”** David Musyj had separately been serving as LHSC’s acting/interim president and CEO before receiving the provincial Supervisor appointment. [ontario.ca], [lhsc.on.ca], [cbc.ca]
Exhaustive list
London Health Sciences Centre → Governance, operations, management practices and concerning financial performance → David Musyj was appointed Supervisor under subsection 9(1) of Ontario’s Public Hospitals Act. The provincial Order in Council states that the appointment was considered in the public interest and that Musyj would serve at the pleasure of the Lieutenant Governor in Council. The Ministry said his mandate included working with LHSC leadership and staff to address concerns identified through a third-party review, including hospital governance and operations, management practices and financial performance. LHSC’s board members stepped down in connection with the governance transition. → September 24, 2024 → Government of Ontario, Order in Council 1219/2024; corroborated by LHSC and CBC News → Order in Council 1219/2024: appointment of David Musyj as Supervisor; LHSC: Strengthening our governance; CBC News: Hospital board resigns as province appoints supervisor [ontario.ca], [lhsc.on.ca], [cbc.ca]
Date clarification
September 24, 2024 is the formal appointment date recorded in the Order in Council. [ontario.ca]
September 25, 2024 is the date LHSC and news outlets publicly announced or reported the appointment. [lhsc.on.ca], [cbc.ca]
A January 29, 2026 Order in Council increased the maximum number of remunerable days for the existing Supervisor from 375 to 875 so he could complete the mandate. It did not appoint another Supervisor, so it is not a separate entry in the list. [ontario.ca], [ontario.ca]
The June 2026 Supervisor’s Interim Report continued to identify David Musyj as LHSC’s Supervisor, supporting that there had not been a replacement by the requested cutoff date of July 16, 2026. [ontario.ca]
Result: 1 provincial Supervisor appointment, David Musyj.
