I found 13 publicly documented leadership, CEO, medical-leadership, and hospital Board of Governors changes at North York General Hospital (NYGH) between July 16, 2021 and July 16, 2026. I treated “board changes” as the hospital’s Board of Governors, not changes limited to the North York General Foundation board. I also included material senior-leadership transitions that directly affect hospital governance and operational accountability.
One caution: some NYGH annual-review documents show the board composition for a fiscal/year-in-review period rather than giving an exact appointment date for each individual governor. Where the exact effective date is not publicly documented, I identify the change as occurring during the documented year/AGM cycle rather than inventing a date.

Hospital → North York General Hospital
Issue → Board of Governors turnover / three governors no longer listed
Paragraph blurb → NYGH’s 2021–2022 Board roster included Cynthia Devine, Sheila Jarvis and Dr. Louise Lemieux-Charles. As of July 16, 2026, the public record supports Dr. Everton Gooden as President and CEO, Mitch Frazer as Chair of the Board of Governors, Paul Truscott as VP Finance, Data Analytics & CFO, Terri Stuart-McEwan as VP Clinical Services/Chief Nursing Executive, Dr. Richard Bowry as VP Medical Affairs, Research and Education, and Serda Evren as Chief Communications and External Relations Officer. Important source-quality note
There is a data-quality discrepancy in one Ontario Health accountability letter dated February 13, 2025, which still names Bert Clark as Board Chair even though NYGH’s governance record and subsequent public materials identify Mitch Frazer as Chair effective June 18, 2024. I regard the accountability-letter reference as a stale/template governance field rather than evidence that Clark remained Chair. (North York General Hospital)
I also found no evidence of an Ontario government-appointed supervisor or other statutory intervention at NYGH during this period; this list is therefore focused on ordinary Board, CEO and senior-leadership succession rather than supervisory appointments.
