1. Board Chair Succession (June 2024)
Hospital: Mackenzie Health
Issue: Appointment of Board Chair Stephanie Zee 
Paragraph blurb: At Mackenzie Health's Annual General Meeting on June 13, 2024, Stephanie Zee was appointed Chair of the Board of Directors, succeeding Fay Lim-Lambie. Zee, a senior financial services executive and former Vice-Chair of the Board, brought extensive governance, technology risk, and operational management experience to lead the hospital network through its post-pandemic strategic expansion.

2. Board Chair Succession (June 2025)
Hospital: Mackenzie Health
Issue: Appointment of Board Chair John Fursey 
Paragraph blurb: At Mackenzie Health's Annual General Meeting on June 19, 2025, John Fursey was appointed as the new Board Chair, succeeding outgoing Chair Stephanie Zee. Fursey, a retired Senior Partner and Vice President at IBM Canada with a deep background in management consulting, financial management, and audit systems, stepped into the role following two years of active committee service on the Mackenzie Health Board. 
Date: June 19, 2025 (Announced June 20, 2025)

3. President & CEO Departure and Interim Appointment (2025)
Hospital: Mackenzie Health
Issue: Departure of President & CEO Altaf Stationwala and Interim Appointment of Mary-Agnes Wilson 
Paragraph blurb: After more than 14 years of executive leadership, President and CEO Altaf Stationwala departed Mackenzie Health to assume the role of President and CEO at Unity Health, effective June 27, 2025. Mary-Agnes Wilson, who previously served as Executive Vice President, Chief Operating Officer, and Chief Nursing Executive, was appointed as interim President and CEO effective July 1, 2025, ensuring operational stability and uninterrupted execution of strategic priorities while a national search was conducted. 
Date: June 27, 2025 / July 1, 2025

4. President & CEO Permanent Appointment (2026)
Hospital: Mackenzie Health
Issue: Appointment of President & CEO Carmine Stumpo
Paragraph blurb: Following a comprehensive national search process, Mackenzie Health appointed Carmine Stumpo as its new permanent President and Chief Executive Officer, effective April 13, 2026. Stumpo transitioned from his role as President and CEO of Orillia Soldiers' Memorial Hospital, succeeding interim CEO Mary-Agnes Wilson to steer Mackenzie Health through its regional growth and expanding academic partnerships, including its role as a lead partner with York University’s School of Medicine.
Date: April 13, 2026 (Announced December 5, 2025)
