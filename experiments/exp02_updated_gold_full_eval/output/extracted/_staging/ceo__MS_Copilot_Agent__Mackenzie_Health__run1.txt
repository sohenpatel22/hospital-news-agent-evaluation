Mackenzie Health leadership and governance changes
Coverage window: July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026, inclusive
I treated “Mackenzie Health” as the hospital corporation governing Mackenzie Richmond Hill Hospital and Cortellucci Vaughan Hospital, rather than automatically including the separately governed Mackenzie Health Foundation. The organization is assigned to Ontario Health’s Central Region in the supplied hospital-region reference document. [Ontario Ho...y Region 1 | Word]
I searched Mackenzie Health announcements, board agendas and minutes, public leadership pages, mainstream and health-sector media, plus available organizational emails, files, meetings, and chats. The list below contains the changes that were explicitly documented by a reliable public source. It is ordered from newest to oldest.
Important scope and completeness notes
July 15, 2021 Fay Lim-Lambie announcement excluded: Mackenzie Health announced Lim-Lambie’s original Board Chair appointment on July 15, 2021, one day before the requested start date. It is therefore outside the July 16, 2021 to July 16, 2026 interval, even though she remained Chair during part of the period. [mackenziehealth.ca]
Mackenzie Health Foundation excluded from the core list: The Foundation is separately governed. Its June 2024 appointment of Mario Paura as Foundation Board Chair was announced alongside Stephanie Zee’s hospital-board appointment, but it is not a Mackenzie Health hospital-corporation board change. [mackenziehealth.ca]
No inferred board movements: Annual agendas show different director rosters over time, including a June 19, 2025 post-AGM roster, but a roster difference alone does not explicitly establish whether someone was newly elected, re-elected, appointed ex officio, or simply absent from an earlier document. I therefore did not convert roster comparisons into unsupported “joined” or “departed” claims. [mackenziehealth.ca], [mackenziehealth.ca]
July 16, 2026 cutoff: A 2026 recruitment notice said a new volunteer director appointment would begin in fall 2026, after the requested cutoff. It is not included as a completed change. [mackenziehealth.ca]
Bottom line: Within the specified five-year window, the most consequential documented changes were the 2025 to 2026 CEO succession from Altaf Stationwala to interim CEO Mary-Agnes Wilson and then Carmine Stumpo, the Chief of Staff succession from Dr. Steven Jackson to Dr. David Rauchwerger, and two consecutive Board Chair transitions from Fay Lim-Lambie to Stephanie Zee and then John Fursey.
