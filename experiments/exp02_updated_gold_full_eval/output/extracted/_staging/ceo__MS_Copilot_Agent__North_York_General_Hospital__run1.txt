Coverage notes
I found no verified hospital-level Board, CEO or Senior Leadership Team change between July 16 and December 31, 2021. Bert Clark’s appointment as Board Chair occurred on June 14, 2021 and was announced June 15, one month before the requested starting date, so it is outside the reporting window. [nygh.on.ca]
I found no verified new governor election announcement for 2023. NYGH published recruitment material, but recruitment itself is not a leadership change and was not counted. [nygh.on.ca]
The February 24, 2026 posting seeking two governors was also excluded as a vacancy notice, but the resulting appointments of Simone Atungo and Dr. Kathryn Nichol are included above. [nygh.on.ca], [miramsbecker.com]
The list is exhaustive to the extent of publicly indexed, date-verifiable announcements. NYGH’s website search indexing sometimes exposes titles without the full historical article body, so I cross-checked those entries with appointment-search firms and official NYGH social or board records. I did not interpret a person’s absence from a current web page as proof of a departure.
