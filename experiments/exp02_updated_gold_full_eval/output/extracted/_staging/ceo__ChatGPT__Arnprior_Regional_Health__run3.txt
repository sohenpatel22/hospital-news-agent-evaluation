Arnprior Regional Health — leadership, CEO and Board changes
Important scope findings
No qualifying change was found between July 16 and July 31, 2021. ARH did announce two new directors on June 23, 2021 — Doug MacCrae and Curtis McGrath — but that announcement falls outside your requested start date and therefore is excluded. (Arnprior Regional Health)
The Board minutes also show that ARH was deliberately reducing its Board composition from 12 to 10 directors during the 2022 governance cycle, reflecting a broader governance restructuring rather than merely individual turnover. (Arnprior Regional Health)
For 2025–26, ARH’s 2024/25 annual report confirms the Board roster during that reporting year as Oliver Jacob, Pete Kenny, Rick Holock, Dr. Christina Veale, Colleen MacPhee, Curtis McGrath, Jacqueline Asselin, Shyam Ramrekha and Beth Ciavaglia, with Oliver Jacob as Chair. It separately identifies Jordan, MacPhee and McGrath as retiring leaders, which is consistent with the subsequent Board roster containing Nicholson, Balmer and Blais. (Arnprior Regional Health)
I did not include post–July 16, 2026 changes. This matters because later August 2026 reporting identifies Pierre Noel as Interim President & CEO of ARH; that is outside your specified period and therefore excluded. (Billy Denault)
