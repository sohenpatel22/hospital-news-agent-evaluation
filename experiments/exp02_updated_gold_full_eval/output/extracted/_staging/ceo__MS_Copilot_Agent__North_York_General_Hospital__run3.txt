North York General Hospital: leadership, CEO and board changes
Review period: July 16, 2021 to July 16, 2026, inclusive
I treated “hospital leadership” as publicly announced changes to North York General’s President and CEO, senior executive leadership team, and hospital Board of Governors. I separated Foundation-board appointments because the Foundation is a distinct corporate board. North York General Hospital is listed in the Toronto region in the supplied regional reference document. [Ontario Ho...ion 1.docx | Word]
The chronology below is ordered from most recent to oldest. It is based primarily on North York General announcements and its year-in-review materials, supplemented by executive-search announcements and Canada Newswire where the hospital page was not fully indexed. Annual reports were also checked to reduce the risk of missing changes that did not receive a standalone news release. [nygh.on.ca], [nygh.on.ca], [nygh.on.ca]

Coverage and limitations
Date boundary: Bert Clark’s appointment as Board Chair occurred on June 14, 2021 and was announced June 15, 2021, both before the requested July 16, 2021 start date. I therefore excluded it from the main chronology, even though he remained Chair during part of the review period. The source states that he succeeded Samuel Elfassy, whose three-year term ended June 14, 2021. [nygh.on.ca]
2026 recruitment: The February 2026 search for two Governors is included only as a transition initiative. I did not find a reliable public announcement, dated on or before July 16, 2026, naming the resulting appointees. The current Board webpage shows the 2026–27 officers and Governors, but it does not by itself specify which members were newly elected in June 2026 or their exact election dates. [nygh.on.ca], [nygh.on.ca]
Exhaustiveness standard: This is an exhaustive list of publicly identifiable, dated appointments, departures and completed-term announcements found through the hospital newsroom, year-in-review reports, current governance pages, Canada Newswire and health-sector executive-search sources. Routine portfolio adjustments, acting assignments or changes mentioned only in non-public Board minutes may not be publicly discoverable.
Mainstream-media check: Searches of mainstream media produced limited independent reporting on these governance changes. Most results were appointment notices or republished hospital releases. Consequently, the most direct and authoritative sources here are the hospital’s own announcements, with executive-search firms used to verify names and effective dates.
