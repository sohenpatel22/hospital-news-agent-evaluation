Findings
I found one publicly documented interim/provincially appointed supervisor for Renfrew Victoria Hospital during July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026: Altaf Stationwala. The appointment began July 8, 2024, following Ontario Health and independent reviews that identified concerns involving hospital governance, management, accountability and financial practices. cite[cbc][mackenziehealth]
Scope note
I found no evidence of another Government of Ontario-appointed interim supervisor for RVH within the specified period. Stationwala’s final investigation later reported financial irregularities and governance deficiencies involving RVH and the affiliated Renfrew Health organization; reporting indicates his supervisory work continued into June 2025. cite[ctvnews][cbc][renfrewtoday]
The legal basis is the Public Hospitals Act, under which the Lieutenant Governor in Council may appoint a hospital supervisor on the Minister of Health’s recommendation when doing so is considered in the public interest. A supervisor generally has the exclusive right to exercise the powers of the hospital board unless the appointment order provides otherwise. cite[ontario]

His mandate was to work with RVH’s CEO, board and staff to strengthen governance, accountability and financial management, while overseeing the hospital’s response to concerns identified in the reviews.
