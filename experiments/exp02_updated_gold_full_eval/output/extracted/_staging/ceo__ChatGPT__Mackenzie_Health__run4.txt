Mackenzie Health — leadership, CEO and Board changes
Key governance timeline
The most consequential sequence for hospital-performance oversight was:
Altaf Stationwala → Mary-Agnes Wilson (interim) → Carmine Stumpo for the CEO role; Fay Lim-Lambie → Stephanie Zee → John Fursey for Board Chair; and Steven Jackson → David Rauchwerger for Chief of Staff. (Mackenzie Health)
The Board itself underwent substantial renewal through the period: the 2023 cycle added Atul Mehta, Deryn Rizzi and David Steinberg, the October 2023 special meeting added John Fursey and Jack Mintz, and the 2025 cycle added Heidi Francis, Viki Lazaris and Jun Yuan, while several long-serving directors reached term limits or exited. (Mackenzie Health)
2026 board caveat: Mackenzie Health’s public governance calendar confirms an Annual Members Meeting and Board meeting on June 18, 2026, and the current Board page shows John Fursey continuing as Chair, but I did not locate a publicly indexed 2026 Board-minutes/appointment document that reliably establishes additional director turnover through July 16, 2026. I therefore have not inferred 2026 director changes from the current roster alone. (Mackenzie Health)
Excluded boundary item: Mackenzie Health appointed Fay Lim-Lambie as Board Chair on July 14, 2021 and announced it July 15, 2021. Because the appointment occurred before July 16, 2021, I excluded it from the main in-period table, despite its proximity to the boundary. (Mackenzie Health)
For performance-management purposes, the highest-impact governance events are the 2025 CEO succession, 2025 Board Chair succession, 2025 Board renewal, 2025 Chief of Staff succession, and the 2023–24 Board expansion/renewal, because they changed the composition or accountability structure of the governing and executive team rather than merely rotating committee assignments.
