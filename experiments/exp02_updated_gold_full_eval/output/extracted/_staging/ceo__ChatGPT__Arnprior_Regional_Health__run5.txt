I found the following publicly documented leadership, CEO, Chief of Staff, and Board changes at Arnprior Regional Health (ARH) between July 16, 2021 and July 16, 2026. I prioritized ARH’s own announcements, Board minutes/organizational charts, annual reports, and then used municipal/regional or sector sources to corroborate transitions.
One important scope note: ARH’s public material does not provide a single historical register of every management appointment. The list below therefore captures the material leadership and governance changes that I could substantiate publicly, rather than ordinary supervisor/manager hires. I also distinguish an actual appointment/retirement from an organizational-chart change where the latter is the only dated evidence.
Executive-level chronology
The leadership sequence that emerges is:
Eric Hanna → retired September 30, 2021 → Leah Levesque became CEO October 1, 2021 → Levesque retired in 2024 → Jeremy Stevenson began August 18, 2024 → Pierre Noel became Interim President & CEO by July 14, 2026. (Arnprior Regional Health)
For medical leadership, the sequence is:
Dr. Christine Schriver → Dr. Florin Padeanu, effective January 1, 2022 → Dr. Kathi Kovacs under the ARH/QCH joint Chief of Staff model, effective August 1, 2025, while Padeanu continued as a practicing physician/hospitalist. (Arnprior Regional Health)
For Board leadership, the key sequence is:
Cathy Jordan served as Chair from 2021–2024 → Oliver Jacob became Chair following the 2024 AGM, with a term through June 2027. Jordan remained a director before subsequently leaving the Board in 2025. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Governance observations relevant to hospital performance management
The most consequential governance changes are the 2021 CEO succession, 2022 medical leadership change, 2022 and 2023 Board turnover, 2024 CEO/Board-chair succession, 2025 move to a joint Chief of Staff model, and the July 2026 interim CEO transition. These are the changes most likely to matter when interpreting shifts in ARH performance, quality governance, financial stewardship, capital delivery and physician-management relationships. ARH itself states that its Board is accountable for strategic planning, financial stewardship and quality-of-care performance monitoring. (Arnprior Regional Health)
One particularly material governance development was the 2023 ONCA-related restructuring of the Board composition: ARH changed its governance framework so that the Board consists of elected directors plus the CEO, Chief of Staff, President of Medical Staff and Chief Nursing Executive as ex-officio, non-voting directors. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Important cutoff point: I have excluded changes announced after July 16, 2026, even where later reporting makes the chronology clearer. For example, ARH’s August 2026 communications provide additional confirmation of Pierre Noel’s interim CEO role and the Board’s 2026 retirements, but those later announcements are outside your specified date range. The July 14, 2026 Longwoods transition entry is therefore the appropriate in-window source for the Pierre Noel transition. (Longwoods)
