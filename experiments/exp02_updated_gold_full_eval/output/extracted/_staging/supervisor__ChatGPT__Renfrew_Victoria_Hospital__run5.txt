I found one formal Government of Ontario hospital-supervisor appointment for Renfrew Victoria Hospital (RVH) during July 16, 2021–July 16, 2026. The appointment was made under s. 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The Ontario Ministry of Health’s current records identify Altaf Stationwala as the supervisor appointed in June 2024, and the termination order confirms that this appointment ended on June 20, 2025. (Ontario)
Appointment chronology
1. Altaf Stationwala — supervisor, Renfrew Victoria Hospital
The formal Order in Council was approved and ordered June 26, 2024. It expressly states that the Minister of Health recommended a supervisor under s. 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act, that the Board had received at least 14 days’ notice, and that the Lieutenant Governor in Council considered a supervisor to be in the public interest. Stationwala was appointed to serve at the pleasure of the Lieutenant Governor in Council. (Ontario)
The supervisor’s final report provides the useful operational distinction: the appointment was announced June 27, 2024 and commenced July 8, 2024. The report says the decision followed ongoing discussions among RVH, Renfrew Health, Ontario Health East and the Ministry of Health, together with a confidential KPMG independent review. (Ontario)
The appointment was subsequently terminated June 20, 2025 by OIC 766/2025. That order specifically references the 2024 appointment and terminates it under s. 9(4) of the Public Hospitals Act. (Ontario)
Why I am treating this as exhaustive
I found no other Ontario government appointment of a formal hospital supervisor for RVH between July 16, 2021 and July 16, 2026. The Ministry of Health’s hospital-supervisor records specifically identify Stationwala as RVH’s supervisor in June 2024, while the Ontario OIC record provides both the appointment and its termination. (Ontario)
There are, of course, other interim/acting hospital leadership changes at RVH during the period—for example, Pierre Noel became interim CEO after Julia Boudreau’s retirement—but those are hospital leadership appointments, not supervisors appointed by the Government of Ontario under the Public Hospitals Act, so I have excluded them from this list. (renfrewtoday.ca)
Bottom line: 1 government-appointed hospital supervisor at Renfrew Victoria Hospital in the requested five-year window: Altaf Stationwala.

Renfrew Victoria Hospital | Governance, financial practices, leadership and operational oversight; concerns arising from financial transfers and management practices involving Renfrew Health | In June 2024, Ontario appointed Altaf Stationwala, then President and CEO of Mackenzie Health, as supervisor of RVH. The province said the appointment was intended to strengthen the hospital’s governance, accountability and ability to deliver care, including addressing concerns about governance and management practices and financial practices identified through an Ontario Health review and an independent third-party review. Stationwala’s final report states that concerns had emerged over approximately $6.595 million in transfers from RVH to Renfrew Health, prompting further review. His mandate examined four dimensions: financial practices, operations/organizational stability and quality of care, leadership competencies and capabilities, and board governance. The final report also identified a need for governance renewal, a new CEO and senior management team, and reunification of Renfrew Health with RVH.
