Scope and validation
I treated “leadership” as changes involving the President and CEO, acting or interim CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair, and elected or departing voting directors. The review covers July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026, inclusive, and excludes committee-only appointments, routine re-elections where no change was announced, Foundation/Auxiliary governance, and changes announced before July 16, 2021.
The internal regional directory places Arnprior Regional Health in the Ontario Health East Region.
