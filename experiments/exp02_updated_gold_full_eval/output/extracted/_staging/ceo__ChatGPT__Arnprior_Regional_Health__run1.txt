Below is the consolidated public-record chronology I could substantiate for Arnprior Regional Health (ARH) from July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026. I treated “leadership change” broadly to include the President/CEO, Chief of Staff, Board Chair, and elected/appointed Board Directors, while excluding routine committee-role changes unless they changed the governing leadership structure.
Board succession picture
The most clearly documented Board evolution is:
2021: Cathy Jordan becomes Chair.
2022: Board recruitment cycle; Beth Ciavaglia is explicitly approved for a three-year term, with Colleen MacPhee and Curtis McGrath joining the Board during the 2022 cycle.
2023: Erin Stitt-Cavanagh and Bob McGaraughty retire; Borys Koba, Rick Holock and Pete Kenny are recommended as replacements, while Oliver Jacob and Colleen MacPhee are renewed. (Arnprior Regional Health)
2024: Oliver Jacob succeeds Cathy Jordan as Chair for a three-year term to June 2027. (Arnprior Regional Health)
2025: Cathy Jordan, Colleen MacPhee and Curtis McGrath conclude their Board service. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Important scope note
I found strong public evidence for the leadership changes above, but ARH does not appear to have an indexed historical “Board changes” register covering every individual director appointment and departure. Its public Board page is current rather than historical, and some 2022–25 director transitions are documented only through Board minutes, annual reports or later acknowledgements. For that reason, I have not treated every person appearing on the current Board page as a separately verified appointment within the period unless I could tie the appointment to a dated record. ARH’s June 2023 minutes, for example, provide unusually strong evidence because they explicitly identify the retiring directors and proposed replacements. (Arnprior Regional Health)
Also, Pierre Noel’s current status as Interim President & CEO is not included because the publicly indexed evidence places that transition after the July 16, 2026 cutoff: Longwoods lists the transition in its late-July/August 2026 updates, and August 2026 reporting identifies him as interim CEO. (Longwoods)
For performance-management purposes, the most consequential changes in the period are therefore the 2021 CEO succession, 2022 Chief of Staff succession, 2023 Board refresh, 2024 CEO and Board-chair succession, and 2025 joint Chief of Staff model.
