The Ottawa Hospital | PHIPA access rights to electronic-medical-record display formats / screenshots | PHIPA Decision 340 addressed a patient's request for screenshots or “samples” of 25 programmed displays in The Ottawa Hospital's electronic medical record system, including Storyboard and Snapshot. The hospital offered printed copies of the underlying personal health information but declined to provide screenshots. The IPC found that screenshots could, in principle, qualify as “records” under PHIPA, but held that the hospital did not create or maintain such screenshots and therefore had no obligation to create them to satisfy the access request. The adjudicator also found the hospital had proposed reasonable alternatives for obtaining the underlying information and dismissed the complaint, including the patient's additional allegations concerning the hospital's response to an earlier access request and alleged PHIPA offences.
