Arnprior Regional Health: leadership and governance changes
Review period: July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026
Arnprior Regional Health is listed in the East Region in the supplied Ontario Hospitals by Region document. ARH encompasses Arnprior & District Memorial Hospital, the Grove Long-Term Care Home, and community programs, so I treated leadership changes announced by ARH as applying to the integrated organization. Scope and completeness notes
I excluded ARH’s June 18, 2021 Board Chair transition from Barb Darlow to Cathy Jordan and the July 2, 2021 director announcement because both predate the requested July 16, 2021 start date. [arnpriorre...lhealth.ca], [arnpriortoday.ca]
I included the Chief of Staff transition because the Chief of Staff is a senior hospital leadership and medical-governance position, even though it is not a CEO or elected board seat.
I included Maggie Harbert’s 2022 Quality Committee appointment but did not characterize her as a corporate board director, because the official release identifies her specifically as a committee member.
Routine annual re-elections, committee reshuffling, changes visible only by comparing undated web biographies, and management-level personnel changes without a dated public announcement were not treated as verified leadership changes.
No additional reliably dated 2024 board-director appointments were found beyond the Board Chair transition, and the 2025 annual report identifies retiring directors but does not provide individual departure dates. Therefore, “exhaustive” here means exhaustive against the publicly accessible, date-verifiable sources located during this review, not undisclosed personnel or in-camera governance actions.
