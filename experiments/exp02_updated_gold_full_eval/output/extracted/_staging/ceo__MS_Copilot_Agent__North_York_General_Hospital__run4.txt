North York General Hospital: leadership and governance changes
Coverage period: July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026
Order: most recent to oldest
Boundary and completeness notes
No in-range 2021 leadership change was located. Bert Clark’s appointment as Board Chair occurred on June 14, 2021 and was announced June 15, 2021, one month before the requested starting date of July 16, 2021, so it is excluded from the list. [nygh.on.ca]
Foundation governance is excluded. The June 2025 release announced six total board appointments, but only Pavi Binning joined the hospital Board of Governors. Sean Cohan, Christine Cruz-Clarke, Ted Garrard, Vince Imerti and Richard So joined the separate North York General Foundation Board. [newswire.ca]
2026 governor recruitment is not counted as a completed change. NYG posted a February 24, 2026 search for two governors for the board year beginning June 2026, but I did not locate an authoritative public announcement identifying the successful candidates by the July 16, 2026 cutoff. [nygh.on.ca]
The current board page identifies the 2026–27 officers as Mitch Frazer, Chair; John Walsh, Vice Chair; Kristina Fanjoy, Treasurer; and Mark O’Connell, Secretary. It supports the current endpoint roster, but it does not itself provide appointment dates for every member, so undated roster differences have not been presented as historical “changes.” [nygh.on.ca]
This is an exhaustive public-source list to the extent verifiable from indexed official hospital communications, annual reports and corroborating executive-search or healthcare-industry notices. NYGH’s website was the primary source, but several older pages did not expose their complete article text to the search index, so the corresponding Mirams Becker, Boyden or industry reproduction was used where it supplied the names and effective dates.

North York General Hospital | Chief Communications and External Relations Officer appointment | Serda Evren appointed to the senior leadership team. NYG appointed Evren as its next Chief Communications and External Relations Officer, effective January 26, 2026. The portfolio covers internal and external communications, issues and crisis management, government and community relations, and brand and reputation. She previously held senior communications, brand and chief-of-staff roles at Mastercard, following earlier work with the Ontario government and Rogers Communications. North York General Hospital | Vice President, Medical Affairs, Research and Education appointment | Dr. Richard Bowry appointed to a new senior leadership role. Bowry began as Vice President, Medical Affairs, Research and Education on April 1, 2025. The appointment consolidated leadership of NYGH’s medical, education and research mandates. He had previously served in NYGH roles including Chief of Anaesthesia, Chair of the Credentials Committee and Vice Chair of the Medical Advisory Committee. North York General Hospital | Vice President, Clinical Services and Chief Nursing Executive appointment | Terri Stuart-McEwan appointed to lead clinical services and nursing. Stuart-McEwan was selected as NYG’s Vice President, Clinical Services and Chief Nursing Officer/Executive, beginning February 11, 2025. The role oversees acute and specialized clinical services, nursing professional practice and interprofessional care teams. She joined from Oak Valley Health, where she was Vice President, Clinical Programs and Chief Nurse Executive. North York General Hospital | Board chair succession | Mitch Frazer succeeded Bert Clark as Chair of the Board of Governors. NYGH publicly welcomed Frazer as its new Board Chair in June 2024. The hospital’s sponsored appointment notice identified him as Chair of the Board of Governors, Managing Partner of Mintz’s Toronto office and Chancellor of Ontario Tech University. NYGH’s 2024/25 report subsequently listed Frazer as Chair, while the preceding 2023/24 report listed Bert Clark. North York General Hospital | Four new hospital board governors | Jonathan Mackey, Gillian Akai, Kristina Fanjoy and George Mavroudis elected. At NYGH’s June 17, 2024 Annual General Meeting, the four were elected to the Board of Governors. The announcement described Mackey as founder of Mackey & Company, Akai as a senior Postmedia legal and administrative executive, Fanjoy as CPP Investments’ Chief Financial Officer, and Mavroudis as President and CEO of Guardian Capital Group Limited. [miramsbecker.com] | June 17, 2024, election; announced June 18, 2024 | North York General Hospital; Mirams Becker | NYGH welcomes four new Board Governors
North York General Hospital | President and CEO succession | Dr. Everton Gooden appointed President and CEO. NYGH announced Gooden as its next President and CEO on September 29, 2023, with a start date of December 1, 2023. Before the appointment, he had spent more than 20 years at NYGH and had held roles including Chair of the Medical Advisory Committee, President of the Medical Staff Association, Division Chief of Head and Neck Surgery and member of the NYGH Foundation Board. [nygh.on.ca] | December 1, 2023, effective date; announced September 29, 2023 | North York General Hospital | Dr. Everton Gooden Appointed New President and CEO
North York General Hospital | President and CEO retirement and transition | Karyn Popovich announced her retirement. Popovich informed the Board of Governors that she would retire following a hospital career of more than 40 years. The March 15, 2023 report stated that she would continue leading NYGH until a new President and CEO was in place, then anticipated for mid-fall 2023. Gooden’s subsequent appointment completed that succession process. North York General Hospital | Three new governors and one new board-community member | Robert Sankey, Dr. Christine Williams and Toni Rossi elected as Governors; Divya Khan joined as a community member. At the June 13, 2022 Annual General Meeting, Sankey, Williams and Rossi were elected to the Board of Governors. The board also welcomed Khan as a community member. The contemporaneous announcement described Sankey as CEO of Burgundy Asset Management, Williams as an immunologist and OICR executive, Rossi as Infrastructure Ontario’s President of the Real Estate Division, and Khan as a senior infrastructure-investment professional.
