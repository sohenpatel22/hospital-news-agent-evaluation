London Health Sciences Centre → David Musyj, Supervisor | Governance, operations, management practices and financial performance | The Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed David Musyj of LaSalle as Supervisor of London Health Sciences Centre after the Minister of Health recommended supervision and provided the hospital board with the required notice. Contemporary reporting said the appointment followed a third-party review that identified concerns involving hospital governance and operations, management practices and financial performance. CBC reported that LHSC was projecting a $150-million budget deficit and that the hospital’s board resigned in connection with the transition to provincial supervision. Musyj’s mandate included working with LHSC leadership and staff, reporting regularly to the Ministry of Health and addressing governance, financial and operational stabilization. LHSC’s own announcement described the objective as strengthening governance, accountability and the organization’s ability to deliver care. [ontario.ca], [cbc.ca], [lhsc.on.ca] | September 24, 2024. This is the legal effective date in Order in Council 1219/2024; the appointment was publicly discussed by LHSC and CBC on September 25, 2024.
