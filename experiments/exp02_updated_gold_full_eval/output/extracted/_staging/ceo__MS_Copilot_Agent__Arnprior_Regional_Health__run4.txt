Arnprior Regional Health leadership and governance changes
Coverage period: July 16, 2021 to July 16, 2026
I reviewed official Arnprior Regional Health releases, annual reports, board minutes, recruitment material, and local-media coverage. Because Arnprior Regional Health includes Arnprior & District Memorial Hospital, The Grove Long-Term Care Home, and Community Programs, the list below covers changes at the ARH corporate leadership and governing-board level, including hospital medical leadership. Scope and completeness notes
No July 2021 board appointments are included. ARH’s appointment of Doug McCrae and Curtis McGrath, and the departures of Don Carmichael and Ryan Lunney, were announced on June 23, 2021, before the requested July 16, 2021 start date. [arnpriorre...lhealth.ca]
Routine committee assignments and simple meeting attendance changes were excluded. I included elected directors, Board Chair and Vice-Chair changes, CEO transitions, senior executive appointments explicitly identified in public records, and Chief of Staff changes.
2025 board dating is less granular than other years. The 2024/25 annual report clearly identifies retiring leaders and the board roster, but the retrieved official material does not provide a separate election date or term length for Dr. Christina Veale, Jacqueline Asselin, or Shyam Ramrekha. I have therefore reported only what the annual report directly establishes. [arnpriorre...lhealth.ca]
