1. Stevenson Memorial Hospital → Governance, management, quality-of-care and financial concerns → Eric Hanna → September 26, 2024 → Government of Ontario, Order in Council 1271/2024 → Order in Council 1271/2024
Paragraph blurb: Following a Ministry-appointed investigation into the hospital’s governance, management and quality of care, the Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed Eric Hanna as Supervisor under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act. The appointment was intended to provide oversight of hospital operations and support corrective changes addressing governance, accountability, financial stability and patient care concerns. The underlying investigation produced 16 recommendations, including the appointment of a Supervisor. Hanna was required to report progress to the Ministry of Health and Ontario Health. His appointment took effect on September 26, 2024 and was revoked at the end of June 8, 2025, when he stepped down and was succeeded by Carmine Stumpo. [ontario.ca], [ontario.ca], [newtectimes.com]

2. Stevenson Memorial Hospital → Continuation of governance, accountability, financial and patient-care improvements → Carmine Stumpo → June 9, 2025 → Government of Ontario, Order in Council 767/2025 → Order in Council 767/2025
Paragraph blurb: After Eric Hanna indicated that he wished to step down, the Lieutenant Governor in Council appointed Carmine Stumpo to continue the Supervisor’s duties. The Order in Council states that the appointment was considered to be in the public interest and made Stumpo the successor Supervisor effective at the first instant of June 9, 2025. His mandate continued the work arising from the investigation, including strengthening governance and accountability, improving finances and supporting the hospital’s ability to deliver patient care. Stumpo’s January 2026 interim report confirms that his assessment began June 9, 2025 and describes work on governance renewal, leadership, operational priorities and the hospital’s redevelopment. [ontario.ca], [ontario.ca], [bradfordtoday.ca]
