Board-cycle continuity findings

2022: Fay Lim-Lambie, Stephanie Zee, Azi Boloorchi, Peter Hargitai and Joby McKenzie were recommended/elected for additional three-year terms; Fay remained Chair and Zee Vice-Chair. (Mackenzie Health)
2023: Atul Mehta, Deryn Rizzi and David Steinberg were newly elected, while Fay remained Chair and Zee remained Vice-Chair; Joby McKenzie became Second Vice-Chair. (Mackenzie Health)
2024: Joseph Amato and Dev Chopra/Ruby Philip-Katyal-related continuing terms were handled through the reappointment cycle; the Board simultaneously effected the Zee chair succession. (Mackenzie Health)
2025: Jun Yuan, Azi Boloorchi, Joby McKenzie and Peter Hargitai received three-year terms; John Fursey became Chair and Joby McKenzie Vice-Chair. (Mackenzie Health)
2026: I found no additional Mackenzie Health Board membership or Chair turnover publicly documented through July 16, 2026. The organization's 2026 Board schedule shows the June 18 annual meeting, while the current Board roster continues to identify John Fursey as Chair. (Mackenzie Health)
