Renfrew Victoria Hospital | Altaf Stationwala appointed as hospital supervisor following governance, management and financial-practice concerns | Following reviews by Ontario Health and an independent third party, the province appointed Altaf Stationwala to oversee RVH and work with its CEO, board and staff on concerns involving hospital governance, accountability and management practices, including identified financial issues. The Lieutenant Governor in Council formally appointed him under subsection 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act, finding the appointment to be in the public interest. The appointment order was approved on June 26, 2024; Mackenzie Health stated that his operational role would begin July 8, 2024. His final report, dated April 24, 2025, described the original concern as the relationship between RVH and the related entity Renfrew Health. The government subsequently terminated the appointment effective 11:59:59 p.m. on June 20, 2025.
