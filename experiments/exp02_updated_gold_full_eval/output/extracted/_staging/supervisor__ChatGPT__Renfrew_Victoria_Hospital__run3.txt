Exhaustiveness check
The primary Ontario sources support only this one government-appointed supervisor for RVH during the requested period:
Altaf Stationwala was appointed under s. 9(1) of the Public Hospitals Act by O.C. 886/2024, approved June 26, 2024. The order expressly states that the Lieutenant Governor in Council considered a supervisor to be in the public interest. (Ontario)
Ontario’s Ministry of Health page identifies Stationwala as the Renfrew Victoria Hospital supervisor appointed in June 2024 and provides his final report. (Ontario)
His appointment was subsequently terminated by O.C. 766/2025, effective June 20, 2025. (Ontario)
I found no additional Ontario Order in Council appointing a supervisor to Renfrew Victoria Hospital from July 16, 2021 through July 16, 2026 in the government records I checked.
One date nuance is worth flagging for performance-management purposes: the legal appointment date is June 26, 2024, whereas Mackenzie Health’s contemporaneous announcement said Stationwala’s supervisor role would begin July 8, 2024. (Ontario)
The final supervisor report is particularly relevant to hospital-performance oversight because it records findings across financial, operations, leadership and governance dimensions and describes a subsequent turnaround involving a new senior management team and CEO. (Ontario)

Renfrew Victoria Hospital | Governance, management and financial-practice concerns involving Renfrew Victoria Hospital and related entity Renfrew Health | The Ontario government appointed Altaf Stationwala as supervisor after the Minister of Health recommended the appointment and the Lieutenant Governor in Council determined that it was in the public interest. Ontario’s final supervisor report says the appointment arose from concerns about the relationship between RVH and Renfrew Health, including the appropriateness of hospital funds transferred to that related entity. The review subsequently identified a broader series of irregular transactions and practices involving certain former executives, and Stationwala’s mandate included strengthening financial controls, governance, leadership and accountability. The report states that no current active RVH executive or current board member was involved in initiating the identified irregularities.
